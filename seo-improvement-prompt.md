# SEO Improvement Prompt — Dev Academy

Approved appendix for all public SEO work on Dev Academy.

## Brand spelling

- Always write **Dev** (never "Div").
- Arabic name: **ديف اكاديمي** / **ديف أكاديمي**.

## Priority keywords

- **ENE.islam abdo** / **islam-abdo-1**
- Local: **Dev Academy FCAI Damietta** / **ديف اكاديمي FCAI دمياط**

## Secondary keywords

- **Dev Academy Egypt** / **ديف اكاديمي مصر**

## Application rules

- Use keywords naturally in titles, descriptions, headings, and body copy only where they are relevant to the page.
- Keep one clear search intent per page and unique titles/descriptions for every public route.
- Non-home titles end with the platform name: `| Dev Academy`.
- Use the production origin `https://dev-academy.pages.dev` for canonicals, sitemap, and structured data.
- Arabic and English variants may appear together when natural; do not repeat keywords across every page.
- Only use real, verifiable links (GitHub, LinkedIn, social profiles). Never invent profile URLs.

## No official affiliation

- Dev Academy is a student platform for students of the Faculty of Computers and Artificial Intelligence (FCAI), Damietta National University (جامعة دمياط الأهلية).
- It is **not** an official university or faculty platform and does not represent them.
- Do not use the university logo or imply endorsement.
- Keep the explicit wording: منصة طلابية لطلاب كلية الحاسبات والذكاء الاصطناعي (FCAI)، جامعة دمياط الأهلية — وليست منصة رسمية تابعة للجامعة ولا تمثلها.

## Quality and ethics

- No keyword stuffing, hidden text, cloaking, doorway pages, or fake pages.
- No ranking guarantees or promises.
- Public pages must stay useful, accurate, and accessible; private routes stay `noindex`.
